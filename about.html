Jelin Raphael Akkara

I am pursuing a PhD in the BMCS programme at the University of Padua (UniPD), in collaboration with the Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK). My research in Embodied AI investigates how autonomous agents can understand complex environments, identify the causes of failure, and adapt their behaviour to recover robustly. I am particularly interested in multimodal reasoning, vision-language models, memory, personalization, and causal and counterfactual approaches to intelligent interaction in 3D environments.
